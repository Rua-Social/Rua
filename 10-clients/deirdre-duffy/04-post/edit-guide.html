Rua SocialThe method
Read this once

Two picture timelines. Then copy them.

You are not cutting 28 different videos from scratch. You cut the pictures once per kitchen. Every extra film is a copy of that timeline with a different voice on it, and a different shot at the start so it does not look like the same post.

Timeline 1 · Swords
Gareth’s kitchen. Oak and grey.

Clips 1475 to 1482, plus Gareth on 1473 and 1474. Covers her voiceover, all of Gareth, and the short slices of her script.

Timeline 2 · Calderwood
The green kitchen. Vaulted ceiling.

Clips 1483 to 1498. Covers her voiceover, the lighting, Dekton, the drawer, the floor, the hall.

How she should look

She was upset after the first takes. Do not lead with her talking to camera. Use the voiceovers she wrote later, and use Gareth. Show the rooms. If you use her voice from the first takes, put pictures over it.

Export

New FCP project: 1080 by 1920, 25fps. Spatial Conform Fill. Crop vertical off the 4K. Pan on the long moves (1480, 1490, 1498). Burn captions on every film. First line of text must make sense with the sound off. One file per film. Post that file to Instagram and to TikTok.

Do this in order

Sit down and cut in this order.

  1. Open the First shoot event. Make a 1080 by 1920 project. Do not start a new library.
  2. Build the Swords B-roll timeline. About 90 seconds of the nice pictures. That is Timeline 1.
  3. Build the Calderwood B-roll timeline. About 90 seconds. That is Timeline 2.
  4. Duplicate Timeline 1. Put her Swords voiceover on it (the m4a, skip “Kitchen number one”). Burn captions. This is the first film we sold.
  5. Duplicate Timeline 2. Put her Calderwood voiceover on it, take 4 only. Skip the slates. Burn captions. This is the second film we sold.
  6. For each extra film below: duplicate the right picture timeline, drop in that voice, change the first shot, export.
  7. Stills last. Rotate the ones that came in sideways. 1504 is the quiet portrait. Not 1505, her eyes are closed.
