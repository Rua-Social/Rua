The trick

Two spines. Then steal them.

Do not make 28 picture edits. Make two. Every extra film is a duplicate timeline with a new voice and a different first frame. For the audience that is a new post.

Spine A · Swords
Oak, island, Gareth’s house

P1001475 to 1482. About 90 seconds of selects. Covers the sold VO, every Gareth short, and the Swords VO slices.

Spine B · Calderwood
Green, terracotta, the vault

P1001483 to 1498. Covers the sold VO, lighting, Dekton, doors, drawer, floor, stained glass.

Rule for this job

Do not force talking-head. Hero audio is the VOs she wrote, and Gareth. Picture is the rooms. Keep her when she is confident. Never when she is interrupted, coached, or upset.

One file, two apps

Every master is 9:16, 1080×1920, 25fps, burned captions, mute-proof first line. Post the same file to Instagram Reels and TikTok. No 4:5. No LinkedIn recut. TikTok likes the 12 to 25 second harvest. Instagram can take the 60 second sold films and the shorts.

First frame of a harvest cut must not match the sold film it was stolen from. Change the open shot. Stagger posts by about ten days if two films share a spine.
