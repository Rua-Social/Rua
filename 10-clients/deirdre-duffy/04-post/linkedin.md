## Gareth on LinkedIn

Yes, after the Swords film, not as post 1.

LinkedIn is a decision feed. A homeowner explaining why they did not build is more useful here than her VO. He is the one talking-head this run can carry. She is not.

Frame it as the brief, not a testimonial. Caption names the problem. Then he speaks. Cut starts on the room, then him. No logo, no stars, no "hear from our clients", no Dee Design sting.

Use only words he said. Confirm he is happy to appear on her LinkedIn before it goes up. If he is not, the Swords film stands alone and this slot becomes extra Calderwood stills.

## What would hurt her here

Do not open LinkedIn with Deirdre talking to camera, and do not post the 9:16 Reels with Instagram caption energy.

A shaky first talking-head, trending audio, or a hashtag dump will make an eighteen-year kitchen designer look like she is running a content campaign. The people on LinkedIn who hire for this work are weighing a spatial decision against a build. They will bounce.

Volume in the folder. Sequence on the feed. She posts the six. She does not dump the lot in a week.
