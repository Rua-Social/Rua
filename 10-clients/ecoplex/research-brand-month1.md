## The finding that changes the pack

The posts that travel already are the brand book. Castleknock, the EV explainers, the two TikTok outliers, IWD, the mural. Review cards, grant carousels and "no guesswork" stills are the same messages delivered as a leaflet. 1–4 likes. Off-brand in form, not in fact.

Pillar 4, Feel Good Stories, is almost absent as film. The strongest story they own (quoted without seeing the house) has never been a person on camera. Message 3 (one team, no subcontractors) is asserted in copy and not shown as faces. That is month 1. It is also the only asset type that survives a six-second ad cut.

## Tagline law (do not touch)

- Installers of feelgood
- Lockup: "Installers of — feelgood" (two lines, em dash)
- Line: Ecoplex are the installers of feelgood
- feelgood is one word
- Four messages each close "feels good"
- "feels good" is earned by a person or it is not said. Never OST. Never the caption's first line. About one in three posts.
