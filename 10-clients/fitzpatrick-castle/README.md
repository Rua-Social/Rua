## History source (received 18 August)

Tommy sent `History of Castle.doc` (local copy:
`~/Downloads/History of Fitzpatrick Castle.doc`). Short house
history, titled **The History of Killiney Castle**. Usable beats
for Hidden Corners / grandeur prompts:

- Built 1741 by John Mapas. Earlier buildings on the site.
  Killiney Hill Obelisk, 1742, put up as poor relief. Inscription:
  "Last year being hard with the poor walks about these hills and
  this were erected by John Mapas, June 1742." Motto *Nisi Dominus
  Frustra* (Psalm 127).
- 1755 Captain Maunsell. Failed lead mine on the estate.
- 1770 Colonel Loftus. Sold 1772 with 150 acres. Converted barren
  stony soil to meadow and pasture, cut a road around the hill.
- Lord Clonmel, 1790, spent £3,000 improving the estate.
- Robert Warren renovated the original Mapas house, donated land
  and most of the money for Killiney Parish Church (plaque still
  in the church), and ran a flag from the castle to signal he was
  in residence to his other estate, Balldonera in West Wicklow.
- Mrs Chippendale Higgan planted the trees and shrubs still on
  the grounds.
- Paddy Fitzpatrick (Patrick J), born Vico Road, Dalkey, trained
  at the Gresham, bought Killiney Castle in 1970. Flagship of the
  Fitzpatrick Hotel Group. PJ's Restaurant is named after him.
