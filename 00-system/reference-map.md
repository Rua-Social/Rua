# Rua reference map

**Library, not production context.** Do not load this file at the start of a
job chat. Do not import it into a skill. Use it when the human is asking
about the library, or has agreed you may look up a deliverable type. Then
open the original artefact. Never reproduce wording or structure from a
description in this file.

A navigable background library of Rua's previous work, built so a session can
consult real artefacts instead of re-deriving them. Discovery-led: the reverse
index at the end maps the cues that actually come up in conversation to exact
source paths.

Built 14 August 2026 from the recovery audit and Darragh's review of the 36-item sample.
Ten items were read closely. Everything else relevant is catalogued at a lighter level.

**Nothing here is a design canon.** These are records of what was made, what it was for, and
what is worth looking at inside it. Where something is a habit rather than a decision, it is
described as a habit.

## The visual baseline, stated honestly

The recurring look across every HTML and PDF deliverable — Instrument Serif italic headlines,
DM Sans body, `#1a1a2e` navy, `#7c3aed` purple, A4 page divs, purple-left-bordered insight
boxes — **is Claude's baseline document style, not a distinctive Rua design system.** Darragh
identified it as such on sight, and the evidence supports him:

`rua_social_style_brief050526.md` opens with *"Use this brief to recreate the Rua Social
design system in a new chat. Paste the whole thing at the start of your request."* The brief
exists to make Claude reproduce the same output across sessions. It is a prompt-portability
document. The consistency across decks is session-to-session reproduction, not a design
decision defended over time.

`references/document-build.md` in the repo calls this "the full Rua Social design system" and
instructs that it be reused with copy adjusted, not the system. Treat that framing as an
assumption on record, not as fact.

**This does not make the material worthless.** What is genuinely Rua's, and worth studying,
sits above the CSS: the tier logic, the conservative-floor arithmetic, the change-log
discipline, the flag-and-action-list mechanics, the reference device below, and the decisions
about what a client is asked to do. Meaningful departures from the baseline are recorded
against each artefact.

## A1 · Skehans concepts deck, final iteration

**Path** `/Users/darraghhoare/Downloads/skehans_concepts_250526.pdf`
**Client** Skehans Free House · **Date** 25 May 2026 · **Type** Concepts deck ·
**Stage** 4, reissued after Stage 5 pre-production
**Audience** Kasia Fitzsimons, co-owner and sole approver
**Purpose** Single source of truth for the sprint's creative. The schedule and shot list derive
from it.

**Structure (20 pages).** Cover ("Here's what we're going to make", meta grid, `May 2026, v3`)
→ *All concepts at a glance*, overview table grouped by tier → *Output*, a per-concept minimum
asset table ending in a stated conservative floor of 27 → thirteen concept pages → copy bank
across two pages → version history / change log across two pages → sign-off.

**Per concept page.** Tier banner (tier, who is needed, a one-line access note) · pillar label
(PLACE / HOST / GUINNESS / SOCIAL-FIRST) · number, status and type badges · creative title ·
description · **WHY IT WORKS** insight box tied to a real like-count from the research ·
three-cell logistics grid (location / audio / what we need) · numbered shot list where
relevant · question list where voiced · caption-or-format note.

**Visual treatment.** The baseline. Letter-spaced `R U A  S O C I A L` wordmark. Footer
"Rua Social, Confidential" plus page number.

**Revision lineage.** 25 Mar alignment list → 26 Mar first real concept list → 17 Apr
call-note revision → 12 May → **25 May post-reissue** → a 28 May byte-identical re-download.
**Editable source: none.** Only the 26 Mar HTML survives anywhere.

**What to inspect or reuse.**
- The **change log**, which is the strongest thing in the document. Entries carry date,
  concept number, and the reason: *"009 Killed. No replacement. The slot is retired and not
  reused."* Concept numbering holds for the life of the project.
- The **conservative floor arithmetic**: every concept states a minimum, the minimums sum to
  a single stated number (27), and that number is what the delivery ledger later reconciles
  against. This is the spine that connects Stage 4 to Stage 8.
- The **tier ordering** as a client-management device: it makes visible exactly where the
  client's coordination is required, lightest first.
- Concept 004's **visual script page** — a two-column audio-to-visual table cutting picture to
  a Blindboy voiceover line by line. The only storyboard-like artefact in the Skehans set.
- **Sequencing to note:** the sign-off page states the derivation rule explicitly rather than
  leaving it to the skill file.

## A2 · Fitzpatrick Castle concepts deck

**Path** `/Users/darraghhoare/Downloads/fitzpatrickcastle_concepts_160726.pdf`
**Client** Fitzpatrick Castle Hotel · **Date** 16 July 2026 · **Type** Concept list ·
**Stage** 4, reissued after a run-through call
**Audience** Alicia, Tommy and Mark — three approvers, against Skehans' one
**Purpose** Locked concept list post run-through, with every agreed change logged.

**Structure (21 pages).** Cover ("Show, don't tell", `Draft 3 · 16 July 2026`) → **THE
DIRECTION** → **THE LIST** → **THE WINDOW** → concept pages → copy bank ×2 → **ACTIONS** →
**NEXT** → **APPENDIX · CHANGE LOG** ×3.

**Departures from the Skehans template — this is where the practice has moved.**
- **A direction page.** Three named feelings (Curiosity, Charm, Grandeur) with a rule
  attached: *"Each piece should land at least one of them. If a piece doesn't, it isn't for
  us."* An editorial filter, absent from Skehans.
- **A `REFERENCES` block per concept.** See the finding above.
- **A `WINDOW` page.** A dated timeline of what the shoot hangs off — site works, a key
  approver's holiday, paternity leave. Skehans had nothing equivalent.
- **An `ACTIONS` page** with owner and by-when against each open item, ending: *"Anything here
  that can't land before the 21st, say so on the call and we re-sequence around it rather than
  find out in August."*
- **Richer status vocabulary.** Not just GREEN/AMBER but PENDING REFRESH, READY, ROSTER
  PENDING, BACK-POCKET, TO CONFIRM, DATE TBC, STOPS PENDING, INSTRUCTOR PENDING.
- **A PIECES column** quantifying output per concept in the overview table.
- **Change log promoted to a three-page appendix** with a Process row recording a working
  preference: *"No written pre-read before Call 2, by preference. Concepts are presented
  live."* That contradicts the skill's instruction to send a pre-read 24 to 48 hours ahead.
- Plain `RUA SOCIAL` wordmark, co-branded footer `Rua Social × Fitzpatrick Castle Hotel`,
  zero-padded page numbers.

**Revision lineage.** Draft 3. Drafts 1 and 2 not recovered. **Editable source: none**, though
four HTML states of the earlier May Fitzpatrick deck survive (see B4).

**What to inspect or reuse.** The direction page, the reference device, the window page and
the owner-and-deadline actions table. These are four components the current skill does not
have, all of them addressing "does this help the client act".

## A3 · Skehans shoot schedule, post-reissue

**Path** `/Users/darraghhoare/Downloads/skehans_schedule_250526.pdf`
**Client** Skehans · **Date** 25 May 2026 · **Type** Shoot schedule · **Stage** 6
**Audience** Kasia, addressed directly in second person throughout
**Purpose** Block-by-block day plan, with everything the client must confirm flagged and
consolidated.

**Structure (8 pages).** Cover ("Here's the plan for the day", shoot date, wrap time) →
morning / midday and afternoon / late afternoon and evening block pages → questions reference
page → action list → change log → sign-off.

**Per block.** Time range · block title · what is captured · **Who's needed** · an amber
**FLAG** box where the client must do something. Every flag is renumbered into a consolidated
action list on page 7.

**What to inspect or reuse.**
- **Direct address.** *"I'd like to drop my case at the pub before heading to Telegraph Hill."*
  First person singular, second person for the client. No third-person client references.
- **Flag-then-consolidate.** Raised in context, then gathered into one numbered list so the
  client has a single place to work from. The best-designed mechanic in the whole set.
- **Release timings stated per person** — *"Bryan from 11:00, released by 12:30"* — which
  respects the talent's day, not just the shoot's.
- **Human logistics carried plainly**: *"Julius does not work Fridays and is coming in
  specially"*, *"Chai is available after her Friday hair appointment"*.
- **Standing editorial rule repeated** in the client-facing document, not only internally.
- The questions page exists so the client can share them with talent ahead of the day.

**Revision lineage.** 5 May pre-pre-production → **25 May post-reissue** → 28 May duplicate.
The 5 May / 25 May pair is the only direct before-and-after of the reissue protocol operating.
**Editable source: none.**

  a decision — and holding genuinely funny material for outtakes rather than discarding it.
- **Anchor phrases** alongside timecodes, so a cut survives a re-export or a different copy of
  the source.
