# Cinematic treatment

The founder holds the spine and taste. Maker drafts material he can keep, kill
or combine. Treatment development is separate from `rua-shoot-plan` and has no
dependency on it.

Load the engagement through the vault. Keep named people, places, evidence and
approvals in the instance record. The current treatment is the file the
instance file index names. A refinement follows Current file in `AGENTS.md`:
write into that working location, rewrite the index, keep the previous file
off the current list, move it out of the working location into the
earlier-versions place, and update every form the index lists for this film
together. Follow `00-system/communication.md` and the room boundaries in
`00-system/rewrite-contract.md`.

## Working rule

The path is a treatment, a script, a storyboard, and visual aids. Two uses sit
on it. An internal aid is for the founder to iterate and refine. A client-facing
aid is so the client can see the film he means. Discussion comes before a
client-facing document. Build a deck, PDF, board, or other artefact only after
he asks for it.

A request names one stage. A treatment stays prose until a client-facing
package is asked for. When the file index already lists that package with the
prose, a refinement updates every listed form in the same pass. A script, a
storyboard, or a visual aid waits until he asks for that stage. A visual aid
can be asked for beside any stage. It shows the film as it stands. It does
not invent a new one.

The prose shape is in `references/stage-templates.md`. The script, storyboard,
and visual-aid shapes are written with him on the first real use of each.

Carry forward choices already made in the conversation instead of asking for
them again.

## Treatment development

1. Name the purpose and spine from the brief or discussion.
2. Draft the film as short, plain prose, specific about what the audience sees
   and hears. Keep one idea per paragraph.
3. Keep multiple treatments as separate options unless the founder asks for a
   merge. Packaging options does not add a script, cast note, recommendation or
   preferred option.
4. Rewrite what the founder keeps. Preserve open questions only when their
   answers materially change the film.
5. Package the treatment as a client-facing artefact only after an explicit ask.

Use `references/stage-templates.md` for the compact prose shape and checks.

## Treatment pass

Name the purpose and spine from the brief or call. If either is genuinely
missing, ask rather than pitching unrelated films. Offer concrete prose the
founder can react to: who is in frame, what changes, where sound comes from and
where the film releases its tension. A reference describes a feeling or device;
it is not a shot list to copy.

Keep the pass short enough to read aloud. The next pass is a rewrite of what
the founder kept.

## Agreed, open, proposed

Sort every concrete beat before the draft comes back.

- **Agreed.** It is in the brief, the call, or a choice already made in this
  conversation. Name the source in a few words. No source means it is not agreed.
- **Open.** The source offered more than one version, or a fact is missing, and
  the answer changes the film. Leave it open. Do not pick one and disclose the
  alternative afterwards.
- **Proposed.** Added so the founder can hear a complete film. It may sit in the
  prose. The same draft lists it as proposed.

A picture/sound script, a cast note, and a recommendation are separate asks.
They do not ride along with the prose.

On a pass the founder may send onward, `flags` checks the Agreed list against
the source before the draft is treated as ready. Role ownership is in
`00-system/working-roles.md`.

When he asks to pressure this film, call `rua-creative-direction` lane B,
take the pass, and continue here. Do not follow an outside router into a new
concept cycle. A sprint concept page belongs to `rua-shoot-plan`. Keyframes
and a storyboard wait until he asks for pictures, and those pictures return
here.

## Boundaries

- Do not invent quotes, approval, cast availability, dates or locations.
- Do not import another client's world, people or creative device.
- Do not present an unagreed downstream stage, schema, identifier system or tool
  as Rua's established workflow.
- Do not send, book, publish or export without the authorization required for
  that action.
