# Creative direction

This is a pass. One owner calls it. It returns to that owner. It does not
call the owner back, and it does not open the other lane.

Installed skills from outside the repo are source material. `creative-director`,
`image`, and `video` are the ones in play. Open a page for the test below.
Leave that file's later steps, its score, and its required shape unused
unless he asks for that exact thing.

If the page is not on disk, say so. Do not rebuild its method from memory.

`image` and `video` route unfinished work back to `creative-director`, and
`creative-director` starts its own full cycle. Ignore those jumps. The owner
already chose the stage.

## A. Concepting

`rua-shoot-plan` calls this lane. Findings for this client already exist, or
he has asked for a pass on concepts already written. If the findings are
missing, stop and return to Stage 1, unless he has explicitly skipped the read.

Use it to push the list in front of you, or to sharpen a concept already
written. Take these tests:

- The idea in one sentence.
- The insight underneath it, in one sentence.
- A specific feeling, carried by something a person does.
- Swap the client. If the idea still works for someone else, it is generic.
- The first obvious versions get named and dropped.

Return the concepts to the stage that called. A long-list push returns to
the long-list. A deck note returns to that deck. Do not reopen an earlier
stage, and do not open `rua-treatment`.

A concept page in a sprint deck stays in this lane, including when the deck
calls that page a treatment.

## B. A cinematic film

`rua-treatment` calls this lane. The piece is an ad, a music video, or a
short he is already writing. The draft from a brief happens in that skill.
This pass pressures the piece in hand.

Use it to make that piece hit visually and emotionally. Take these tests:

- What the audience sees, and where the sound comes from.
- The feeling, carried by a body, a room, or a sound. A named mood is not enough.
- The image the film ends on.
- A line that could belong to any film gets rewritten until it belongs to this one.

Return the pass to `rua-treatment`. A new campaign is a different ask. Do
not open `rua-shoot-plan`.

Keyframes and a storyboard are pictures of this film. `rua-treatment` asks
for them. A page from `image` or `video` can help make those pictures, then
the pictures come back to the film. They do not start a new idea.

## Either lane

Sourced facts stay sourced. A missing client fact stays missing. Another
engagement's people, places, and devices stay in that engagement.

He decides what is kept. Discussion comes before a client-facing deck, board,
or picture.
