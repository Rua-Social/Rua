# Shortlist

**Objective:** a balanced and informative selection showing the range, evolution, recurring
patterns, limitations and unrealised possibilities in Rua's visual communication so far.

Not a list of best examples. Several items are here *because* they look awkward, superseded,
inconsistent with the stated system, or absent. Rank means likely usefulness for review, not
assumed quality. Nothing below has been opened.

**94 items.** Source categories are kept separate and labelled:
**[R]** Rua-produced historical working material · **[C]** client or collaborator material ·
**[X]** external visual inspiration · **[S]** current skill instructions ·
**[F]** candidate future experiment.

## What the shortlist does not contain

- **No shot list in the skill's stated format.** Deliverable 4 has no exemplar.
- **No HTML source recovered for any concepts deck after 26 March, any schedule, or any
  shot list.** This is a source-recovery gap: the named sources were not found on the
  scanned roots. It does not establish that no editable source exists anywhere (Drive, email,
  another machine, or a chat session's output would not have been seen by this audit).
- **No separately named moodboard, lookbook, style frame or storyboard.** Those four terms
  returned one hit across every root, and it was unrelated. **This is a filename finding
  only. It cannot establish that visualisation material is absent from inside decks or from
  files with generic names** — a concepts deck may well contain reference imagery or style
  frames without saying so in its filename. Carried forward as an open question for the
  content review, not a settled finding.
- **No `creative-library.md`.** Cited five times by the skill, absent from the repository
  and from every scanned root.
- **Almost no client-use evidence.** Six Gemini meeting notes touch a document's reception.
  Everything else would be Darragh's present-day judgement.
