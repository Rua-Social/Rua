Held open. Not resolved here.

## 4. A limit on the research claim

On item 2, the Fitzpatrick Week 0: *"they had less evidence to go on so this part of the
research was heavily backed by my own taste and feeling."*

The skill states that six months of social history is a mandatory minimum and that research
drives the concepts. For the second of two clients, the research was thin and Darragh's taste
carried the direction. The skill does allow for a thin-history client, but it frames the
substitute as competitor and category material, not as the practitioner's judgement. What
actually happened was the latter.
