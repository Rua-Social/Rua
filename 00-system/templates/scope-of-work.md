# Scope of work template

A reusable scaffold for defining any bounded Rua engagement before work is
confirmed. It is model-neutral: it works for bounded execution (shoot-only,
edit-only, subcontract or pickup production), for engagements where Rua owns
the creative response, and for any deliberately bounded ongoing arrangement
such as a retainer with a defined monthly output.

It captures only what every engagement needs settled in advance. It is not a
service menu, a pricing system or a qualification tool. Copy it into the
client's directory under `10-clients/<client>/` and fill it in; delete
guidance notes (in italics) from the working copy.

---

# Scope of work: [client] / [engagement name]

Date: [date]
Version: [v1]

## Engagement summary

*One short paragraph: what this engagement is, in plain language, with its
finish point named.*

## Responsibilities

**Client is responsible for:**

*What the client brings and decides. At minimum: the business need this work
serves, access to people/locations/materials, and timely input at the stages
listed under Dependencies.*

**Rua is responsible for:**

*What Rua produces and manages. State it at the level actually agreed: this
may be execution of supplied concepts, or the full creative response and
production. Say which.*

## Decision owner

*Who has the final say on approving the work, by name. One person. If
approval in practice involves others, name the single person who resolves
disagreement.*

## Deliverables

*The defined body of work, itemised: what, how many, format, and where it
will be delivered. State quantities as a guaranteed floor; exceeding the
floor is at Rua's discretion and creates no obligation.*

|---|---|---|---|
| | | | |

## Inclusions and exclusions

**Included:** *everything covered by the price, including planning, crew,
kit, edit and grade as applicable.*

**Not included:** *name the near-misses explicitly: usage beyond the agreed
channels, additional shoot days, ongoing posting or channel management,
strategy beyond this engagement, anything the client might reasonably assume
is covered but is not.*

## Revisions

*The revision boundary: how many rounds are included per deliverable, what a
round consists of, and that changes beyond that boundary or outside the
agreed concept are handled as an exception (see below).*

## Dependencies and client inputs

*What Rua needs from the client and by when, and what happens to the
timeline if an input is late. List each input with its owner and date.*

## Price and payment

Price: [amount, ex/inc VAT stated]

Payment stages: 50% on confirmation, 50% on delivery of final files, unless
otherwise agreed here. The engagement is confirmed and dates are held only
once the first payment is received.

## Completion and handover

*What constitutes done: the deliverables listed above supplied to the agreed
location in the agreed formats, and any agreed handover material provided.
Completion is delivery of the defined body of work, not the client's
downstream results. State how long delivered files remain available and
where source material lives afterwards.*

## Exceptions and changes

Anything outside this scope is not covered by the price. Additions or
changes are agreed explicitly in writing (email is sufficient) with their
price and timeline impact stated before the work happens. Neither side
should treat unagreed extra work as owed or as precedent.
