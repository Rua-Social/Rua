## What changed in v2 and why

v1 was a strong guide with one gap. Every one of the twenty cuts asks a stranger to care about Papa Rua before they have decided they do. Doubt, grind, origin, craft, collaboration. All good, all conversion material, all aimed at people who already follow.

Nothing on the plan earned the first follow from a cold viewer. That is the top of funnel gap.

v2 adds a light layer of eleven cuts built from banter, mistakes and studio comedy already on the tape. Three reasons this matters more than it looks:

1. **The light cuts need no context.** The nope supercut works for someone who has never heard a Papa Rua song. The origin monologue does not.
2. **The light cuts are mostly dialogue, not music.** That solves the biggest constraint in this campaign. The 15 second cap on unreleased hook exposure is the thing throttling post volume. Eleven cuts that barely touch the songs means eleven more posts without burning any of the new material.
3. **Eight of the eleven are clean.** Most of the strongest cuts in v1 carry f-bombs and cannot go paid. The light layer gives a paid-safe bank for the first time.

**Standing recommendation: two light cuts for every deep cut.** Light runs first in the week, deep runs second. The light one earns the profile visit, the deep one converts it.

**Where we actually are: Turtle Soup is out 28 August, eight days out.** Run the light layer hard between now and release day, because it needs no set-up and it builds reach into the drop. Hold the depth pieces for the fortnight after release, when people have the song and the story means something. That is consistent with what v1 already said about Cut 3.

## Standing rules (apply to every cut)

1. **Verify every quote against audio before overlay.** The captions are auto-generated and rough. Timestamps are reliable, wording is not.
2. **No clean chorus of unreleased material ever airs.** All three songs on this tape are brand new, post-album. Cap intelligible hook exposure at roughly 15 seconds per cut. Process is the product.
3. **Every cut ends on a CTA overlay.** Session footage has no natural spoken CTAs, so it goes in caption and end-frame text. Pre-announce: follow. Post-announce: pre-save Turtle Soup. November batch: Blanka's album link.
4. **Two hard embargoes:** Cut 18 (names the album's opening tracks) waits for announce day. Cut 20 (Tokyo collab reveal) waits for that release. No exceptions.
5. **Language flag:** several cuts carry f-bombs. Fine organic, not for paid. Marked per cut. See the paid-safe bank above for the clean alternatives.
