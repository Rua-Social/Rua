## Brand work done this session

Archetype exercise: core / supported by / elevated by.

Landed, then revised against four sets of lyrics:

| Slot | Archetype |
|---|---|
| **Core** | Everyman |
| **Supported by** | Lover |
| **Elevated by** | Creator |

### How it got there, because the history matters

- First instinct was Everyman core, Hero or Explorer supporting, Creator elevated, with a question about whether Lover belonged.
- **Everyman** was challenged as a defence against seeming pretentious. It survived, but on a different basis: the voice is plain, unelevated, always addressed to a specific "you", concerned with ordinary struggle.
- **Outlaw** was built into the stack on the belief that the songs confess personal guilt and greed. The lyrics do not support this. Guilt is nearly absent. Greed appears in *Travelling* but is judged in other people, not confessed. Outlaw attitude is present (profanity, swagger). Outlaw function is not. Collapsed.
- **Lover** was dismissed and that was wrong. The love child track is Lover as archetype, not love as subject: sensuality, seduction, bodies. The original instinct was correct.
- **Sage** is the live alternative to Lover in the support slot if *Look Out Below* turns out to be more representative than the four-song sample suggests.
- **Creator** remains untested. Lyrics cannot test it.

## Voice note

Use as a filter on all copy.

**Strongest** when concrete and unglamorous:
- "the money's coming slow, no friends on the phone"
- "vultures and crows smell the weak and the old"
- "took it all off black, put it all back on red"

**Weakest** when abstract:
- "in the quiet of the night when the stars are shining bright, a voice is guiding telling me to seize the day"

Generic, wrong register.

---

## Brand as a decision filter

- **Visuals:** no mystique, no aura, no artist alone in a void. Real places, real light, but shot deliberately. If a treatment makes him look elevated above the listener, it fails.
- **Copy:** plain first person. No third-person artist-speak, no genre-defying language.
- **Risk to watch:** Everyman plus a weak Creator becomes a diary entry. The Creator slot is what turns confession into something built.
