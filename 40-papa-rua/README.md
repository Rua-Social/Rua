# Papa Rua

The artist desk: releases, content, live and business. Not Rua Social. Not a client. Not a tool.

Sit here. Open your agent from `~/Rua/40-papa-rua`. The client rooms, the sprint skill, the ship gate and the rewrite contract don't apply in this folder.

**Cut from** `EDIT-DAY.md`. **Post from** `POSTING.md`. If another file disagrees, those two win.

**Plan from** `PLAN.md`. Brand, FMC, career shape, 90 days. Not a cut list.

**Brand answers from** the brand worksheet in the vault. Fiona's questions plus what already landed. Fill it, record it, then we pick the answers apart.

**Live from** `live/`. The 26 November 2026 homecoming is in `live/2026-11-26-homecoming.md`.

Out in 2026: Overthinking (17 April), Turtle Soup (21 August), and Dreamy (11 September), which is Blanka's single with Papa Rua and Nao Yoshioka on Sweet Soul Records.

Detailed documents live in the vault, not Git: lyrics, references, edit guides, the brand worksheet, the France guides, the trial reels hook bank, the PDFs and old session dumps. Open the index with `rua vault get rec-papa-rua`. Camera originals and rendered video stay on Drive or disk.

This folder moved here from its own repository on 19 October 2026, with its history. It stays in the Rua monorepo unless a concrete reason emerges to split it out (separate collaborators, permissions, deployment, or substantial independent development).
