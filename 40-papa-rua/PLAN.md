## Brand, from Fiona Duffy

The deck is `references/Defining your brand as an artist - Fiona Duffy-2_260814_181840.pdf` (14 August). Header on every slide: who you are, how you connect, what you want people to feel.

Brand is not a logo. It is how people perceive you after a song, a Reel, a collab, a room. It becomes a filter:

- Does this feel like me?
- Does this reinforce what I want people to know about me?
- Does this make the right people want to come closer?

### Archetypes (her Core / Supported / Elevated)

| Slot | Word | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Core | Everyman | Evidenced. Plain, a specific you, ordinary struggle. Not her Tesco card. |
| Supported by | Lover | Evidenced. Bodies and a person, not an idea of love. |
| Elevated by | Creator | Aspiration as reputation. Evidenced as making on camera. |
| Collapsed | Outlaw | Attitude exists. Function does not. |
| Watch | Sage | Live in Look Out Below, Turtle Soup, No Wrong. Alternative to Lover only if you justify it against a song. |

Draft sentences, not copy:

- Everyman: I am not above you. I say the ordinary thing to a specific you.
- Lover: The connection is a body and a person.
- Creator: I turn it into a built thing, not a diary.

Risk still live: Everyman without Creator is a diary. Pair fear clips with making.

## Three shapes for twelve months

Rua stays the money. Small client load. Time control. Music video work stays a test, not a Rua service line. No extra org unless a shape names a vendor.

### A. Smallest honest

Finish what is already spoken. Turtle Soup sustain, Nao, then reveal produced sound with Stack E and 34bpm after Nao. No live plan. No new shoot. No manager. Papa Rua is a desk and a posting week. The MV ambition stays named so it does not disappear, even if it does not shoot this year.

### B. Proof year

Same spine as A, plus **one** planned Papa Rua video on a produced single. Treatment, light, decisions before the edit, real place. That piece is art, craft practice, and a reel. Still no Papa Rua staff. Rua specialists only if that one job needs a day. Cost sized so it does not threaten the personal comfort floor. Live is optional and small, after the produced sound is public. Skip the room if it does not exist.

### C. Public musician, still lean

Only if produced sound lands and people actually want rooms. Short Irish run. Still not a tour, still not payroll. A booker is a vendor. If gigs cost more than they return, drop back to B.

**Read:** A is what the desk already is. B is the only extra that honours the video signal without building a company. C is a later year unless live demand shows up unforced.

You pick A or B. C is not this sitting.

### What FMC can decide vs what only you can

They can advise on grants, live pay, whether a publicist is worth it, how export actually turns on, and how to walk IMW as an attendee.

They cannot decide: whether music has to pay for your life (that is the Rua bargain); acoustic then produced sound; whether Rua becomes a music-video company; the artistic half of the diamond; Nao / Melt / album-opener embargoes; Shape A vs B.

Bring them a milestone, not a life. "I am making one produced single and I may shoot a video" is a consult. "What should I be?" is not.
