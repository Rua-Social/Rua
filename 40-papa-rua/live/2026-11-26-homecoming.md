## Eventbrite description

Papa Rua plays a homecoming show on Thursday 26 November to close out the
busiest year of his career so far.

Papa Rua is Darragh Hoare, a songwriter from County Meath now based in Dublin.
He writes songs about finding hope when the world feels like it's coming
apart. His music draws on neo-soul, folk, jazz, hip-hop, shoegaze and skiffle,
and moves between stripped-back acoustic songs and layered studio productions.
Over the past ten years he has gone from self-producing his own records to
playing Electric Picnic and All Together Now.

2026 started with his first London show, at Skehans Free House in Nunhead in
February, in association with Head the Ball. In April he released Overthinking,
the first single from his upcoming debut album, and followed it with Turtle
Soup in August. In September he featured on Dreamy, the new single from French
producer Blanka, alongside Japanese soul singer Nao Yoshioka, out on Japanese
label Sweet Soul Records. In October he played the National Concert Hall with
The Growth Experience.

Next year brings a new sound. Before that, this is a night to hear these songs
live as they are now, and to celebrate the year with the people who've been
there along the way.
