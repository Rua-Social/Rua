## Brand filter (use this, not vibes)

| Slot | What it permits | What it kills |
|---|---|---|
| **Everyman (core)** | Sweat, doubt, lost in the click, dirty feet, "just talk", being performative about not being performative, ordinary places | Mystique, artist-alone-in-a-void, "journey", third-person artist-speak |
| **Lover (support)** | Bodies, softness, "I am a lover", bedsheets, fireside, let go | Generic romance captions, abstract "seize the day" |
| **Creator (elevate)** | Writing live, packing a song like a parcel, varispeed, "I'm cooking", turning a mess into a thing | Diary entries with no making in them |

Personality to aim at: Warm, Provocative, Clever.

How people should feel: understood. Challenged is still aspiration. Do not write "challenged" into a caption unless the clip actually does it.

Voice: concrete and unglamorous. If a line could sit in a generic artist reel, it is the wrong line.

**The algorithm problem this is for:** your own grid currently converts people who already care. Cold viewers bounce. The cuts below have to work on someone who has never heard a Papa Rua song. Entertainment first. Song second. Follow is an overlay, not the hook.

**Rule that creates volume:** dialogue and failure do not burn the 15 second unreleased-hook cap. Performance clips do. Build the funny ones without the chorus, then use the wall take and the 15-second lyric hits as the music layer.
