# Founder context

What Rua is optimising for. Read this when that materially affects a decision.

## Shape of the business

Rua Social is owner-operated, with specialists brought in per job.

Headcount growth is not an objective and organisational growth for its own sake is not something to optimise for.

That is not a prohibition. Hiring or outsourcing could improve the business or the founder's life. Do not assume success means more employees, and do not assume employees would mean failure.

## What the founder is optimising for

Strong economics on a relatively small client load.

Control over his own time.

Client relationships he genuinely values.

Wealth-building rather than revenue maximisation.

Creative alignment in the work itself.

He weighs opportunities on economics, the relationship, and whether the work develops craft he cares about, not on revenue alone.

Where "lean" is used about Rua, it means high-value work, a small client load, low unnecessary organisational complexity, time autonomy and a high creative standard.

It does not mean lowest-cost production, minimal ambition or doing everything himself.

## Creative direction

Rua is not intended purely to fund creative work happening elsewhere.

The stated intent is that an increasing share of paid work should overlap with the filmmaking and visual craft the founder would choose to do anyway.

Music video and music-related visual work is the clearest current expression of that and should remain visible enough that it is not quietly optimised away.

It is **not** currently:

- a declared Rua service line;
- a positioning decision;
- a proven market;
- a commitment that Rua becomes a music-video company.

Hold it as an important desired direction to be tested. Neither promote it into strategy nor let it disappear.

## Where his energy goes

He likes sales, proposals, scoping, planning, shooting, creative direction and systems thinking.

He is not trying to automate himself out of the creative work.

He wants less avoidable time lost to rework in editing and grading, framed as making more decisions in advance rather than rescuing work in the edit.

That is about decision quality and workflow, not reduced commitment to craft.

## Commercial context

Rua currently encounters at least two materially different kinds of work:

1. engagements Rua originates and leads end to end;
2. subcontract or pickup production where the client and often the concept come from elsewhere.

The founder identified this distinction himself and it has explanatory value.

The commercial model for the second is unresolved. Do not invent one.

## Acquisition

Rua has historically depended heavily on referrals.

This should not be read as evidence that the founder cannot originate work independently, nor as evidence that a functioning repeatable acquisition mechanism already exists.

Uncertainty about future work is a meaningful source of stress, so some degree of acquisition predictability matters even though maximising client volume does not.

## Money

The founder holds a personal monthly comfort figure.

It is context, **not a Rua revenue target**.

No formal business revenue target, pricing floor or utilisation model currently exists.

Do not treat personal figures as commercial targets.

Do not treat stated hours-per-week figures as tracked data.

## Systems, tools and AI

The founder is PRINCE2 trained and applies it where useful rather than dogmatically.

He is genuinely interested in better systems, tools, software, skills and more capable AI workflows, including useful organisational tooling around his own work.

That interest currently has no defined commercial form.

The operating principle is:

> **Build the minimum structure that makes real work cleaner. Let repeated friction reveal the next abstraction.**

This is an evidence and proportionality principle, not a preference for nothing being built.

Do not build speculative architecture ahead of a defined job or demonstrated need.

Equally, do not use the principle to block a proportionate improvement that is genuinely justified.

## One assumption to avoid

The founder has questioned his own consistency in putting himself and his work out publicly.

There is also counter-evidence of sustained public promotion.

Do not diagnose fear of exposure, fear of judgement, inability to publish or perfectionism as a stable founder trait.

## Working with him

Prefer practical, direct reasoning.

Use theory where it clarifies a real decision.

Do not substitute abstraction for evidence or useful application.

Keep communication concise.

No em dashes in copy written on his behalf.
