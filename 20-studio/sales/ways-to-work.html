How it starts

Pick a shape. Then we write it down.

Nothing here is a confirmed engagement. The scope of work is the contract. The first payment is the gate.

  1. 1
    Choose a retainer, or a project. If you are unsure, say what you need done and by when. I will recommend one shape.
  2. 2
    We fill a short scope of work. Deliverables, floor quantities, inclusions, exclusions, the decision owner, and the price. Email is enough to agree it.
  3. 3
    First payment confirms the work. Retainers: the first month in advance. A project: 50 percent. Dates are held only once that lands. The balance of a project is due on delivery of the final files.
The floor

Each option names a conservative quantity. Extra output, when it happens, is one-off. It does not raise the next month’s obligation or change the price on its own.

Included
  • Planning, kit, edit and grade for the committed work
  • Captions, on-screen titles, platform formats
  • One revision round per piece, 48-hour feedback windows
  • Usage on your own site and social channels
Not included
  • Ongoing posting or channel management
  • Paid media, boosting, or ads
  • Extra shoot days, or usage beyond your own channels
  • Strategy beyond the agreed engagement
